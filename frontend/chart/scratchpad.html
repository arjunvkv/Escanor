06:00 AM – 09:00 AM 🌅

1. The Morning Audit & Complete Silence

Livermore lived in upstate New York and took an early morning train to Manhattan. He maintained a strict rule: NO ONE was allowed to speak to him before the market opened. No gossip, no tips, no retail chatter.

The Money Market Audit: He examined Call Money Rates, Gold flow shipments between London & NY, and Central Bank balance sheets.

09:30 AM – 03:00 PM ⚔️

2. The Battle: Probing & Sitting Tight

He sat in a private office with only his tape machine and a private quote board. He did not trade constantly. Most hours were spent doing absolutely nothing until price reached his predefined Pivotal Point.

The Probe Test (Small Size): He never went all-in. He placed a small 20% feeler order. If the tape proved him wrong by even 1%, he cut it instantly without hesitation.
The Pyramid Rule: Only when his first order was in profit did he add more. He NEVER averaged down on a loser.
04:00 PM – 07:00 PM 🌙

3. The Evening Ledger & The Bank Vault

After the ticker stopped, everyone else went to the bar. Livermore stayed behind. No clerk was ever permitted to write in his private black ledger—he handwrote every transaction himself.

The Error Autopsy: If he took a loss, he studied it like a crime scene. Did he anticipate the market instead of waiting for the tape?
The Bank Vault Rule: At the end of every winning campaign, he withdrew 50% of the profits in physical cash and locked it into a safety deposit box so the market could never take it back.

The 4 Golden Mental Commandments of Jesse Livermore:

"Do not anticipate the market."
