⚡ 5H FOOTPRINT VISUAL GUIDE
1. Candle Colors (Who Is Trading)
Electric Cyan — Bottom Trap (Buyers Absorbed Selling)
Sellers tried dumping the market, but big institutional limit buys soaked up all contracts. Price refused to fall. Often a bottom reversal.
Burnt Amber — Top Trap (Sellers Absorbed Buying)
Buyers chased higher, but institutional limit sells absorbed every order. Price stalled and capped. Often a top reversal.
Vibrant Emerald — Strong Bull Momentum
Large directional volume pushing price up aggressively. Real buyer control. Do not short this.
Deep Rose / Crimson — Strong Bear Momentum
Heavy selling displacing price down with speed. Real seller control. Do not buy this.
Desaturated Slate — Low-Volume Chop / Noise
Thin retail churn with zero institutional participation. Stay out and protect capital.
2. Lines, Dots & Marks (What They Mean)
White Line inside Body (POC): Point of Control. The exact price where the biggest volume traded in that candle.
Glowing Wick Core: 70%+ of the aggressive volume was absorbed inside this wick. Shows institutional rejection.
White Dot at Wick Tip: Fast rejection velocity. Price probed the high/low and bounced away in under 45 seconds.
