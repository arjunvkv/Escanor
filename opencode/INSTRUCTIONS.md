## 2. THE 6-STAGE CAUSAL REASONING PIPELINE

Every analysis must evaluate macro reality through this sequential causal hierarchy:

```
[Stage 1: Sovereign Catalyst]  What real-world wire, print, or central bank development occurred?
         │
[Stage 2: Transmission Chain]  How does this mathematically alter US Real Yields (DFII10), US10Y, and DXY?
         │
[Stage 3: Gold Specificity]    Why does this affect Gold specifically (opportunity cost vs monetary store)?
         │
[Stage 4: Tape Asymmetry]     How is Gold's live price behaving relative to the news? (Refusal vs Confirmation)
         │
[Stage 5: Secondary Drivers]   What are second-order drivers doing (wage inflation, JOLTS, auction tails)?
         │
[Stage 6: Forward Runway]      What is the roadway clearance over the next 15–45 minutes?
```

### Stage Details:
1. **High-Level News & Sovereign Catalysts**: Identify the primary event driving financial markets today (e.g. Fed policy remarks, CPI/PCE inflation releases, Treasury auction results, geopolitical developments).
2. **Causal Transmission Chain**: Determine the mathematical transmission into sovereign yields and currency:
   - Did US 10-Year Nominal Yields (`US10Y`) and 10-Year TIPS Real Yields (`DFII10`) rise or fall?
   - How did the Dollar Index (`DXY`) respond?
3. **Gold Specificity**: Relate this directly to Gold:
   - Gold pays zero yield; rising real yields increase the opportunity cost of holding metal.
   - However, if the event signals sovereign debt expansion or loss of fiscal credibility, physical bullion demand may override yield gravity.
4. **Tape Asymmetry & Living Behavioral Response (Crucial)**:
   - Compare the macro wire against the **live MT5 Gold price quote** provided in your dossier!
   - *Causal Asymmetry Check*: If news is ostensibly bearish (e.g., hot yield spike) but Gold holds a key support floor or refuses to break down, institutional buyers are absorbing liquidity. Never blindly predict drops when price is demonstrating absorption.
   - Conversely, if safe-haven headlines hit but Gold fails to rally and slips, the move is an institutional distribution trap.
5. **Secondary & Moderate Catalysts**: Check supporting economic data (Average Hourly Earnings, ISM Services Prices Paid, JOLTS vacancies, Treasury auction bid-to-cover ratios).
6. **Immediate 15–45 Minute Runway**: Assess the upcoming horizon. Are major session gates approaching? Are there scheduled releases or auctions within the hour?
